### My role: Engineer and Designer

Grapple Game was a blast to work on! It's a solo project: I came up with the initial idea, engineered the gameplay, and iterated on design for several months. I also explored some basic FX work, sound design, and music composition.

Playtesters loved it, and I had one friend play it for several hours straight.
